1- Redux: A state management library that keeps an app's shared data in one centralized place
and updates it through predictable actions and reducers.

2- Store: The single object holding your entire application's state,
plus methods to read state, dispatch actions, and subscribe to changes.

3- Slice: A modular piece of state (especially in Redux Toolkit)
that groups a feature's initial state, reducers,
and auto-generated action creators together in one place.
